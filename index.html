    THE NIGHT

    There is no last round. Each one sends something different, and each is survived differently. The words at the start of a round tell you what it wants. The board remembers how far you got.

    Dark ghosts
    Keep your light off them. Shoot them or outlast them.
    The Unseen
    Watch for shaking branches and listen for screeches. A lit match or the Spectral video camera reveals their silhouettes. They watch for about ten seconds, then strike from behind. Turn to face them when you hear them close. Outlast them or kill them all.
    The Observer
    Find the bodies and burn each one with a match, before dawn. Burning them wears on your mind.
    The Collective
    When they come, put your flashlight out, then get away from them. A match is allowed.
    The Choir
    They hunt by sound. Go quietly, find the nest, kill what sings.
    The Consumer
    Get the civilians out and kill it before it grows.
    The Slime
    It eats what it rolls over and grows. Nothing you carry kills it. Follow the WATER arrow and lead it into the lake. Shoot the small ones before they cling to your legs.
    The Labyrinth
    Finding the way out wins the round, whatever is in there with you.

    BETWEEN ROUNDS

    Some rounds do not end quietly. If a key appears on the screen, do what it says at once: press it, hammer it, hold it, or touch nothing at all. Getting it wrong ends the night.

    Some rounds do not end quietly. The screen shows you a gesture, and you make it anywhere, at once: swipe the way the arrow points, tap as fast as you can, press and keep pressing, or lift every finger off the glass. Getting it wrong ends the night.

    YOUR GEAR

    Chests are not the only place to look. Some of what stands about can be searched: hold the use key on a car, a crate, a coffin, a hollow stump. What you kill sometimes leaves a bundle where it fell; walk over it. And every round you live through, the night leaves three things and you take one.

    Every chest has parts in it, some of the dead drop them, and each round you survive salvages a few. Spend them at the workbench: batteries, bullets, grenades, and rebuilds of the gun, the flashlight and the radar. The world waits while the bench is open. After the flashlight reaches Mk III, save 35 more parts to replace it with a video camera: its broad green beam lasts longer and can expose writing on walls once.

    Coloured chests can hold kits that fit an upgrade on the spot. The first one you open rebuilds the pistol into a gun that keeps firing for as long as you hold it.

    ARMOR

    Armor is worn, not carried, and lays a shield over your health: it takes every hit first, and grows back once you have gone a few seconds unhurt. There are six kinds, from bone armor (a shield of 25) to the Choir carapace (170). The first three turn up through the early rounds; the heavy ones only once the night has gone on a long while. A better one goes on at once; one no better patches the shield you have.

    YOUR MIND

    Light on a horror wears your sanity down. Hold your breath while standing still to get it back, even when your mind is empty. A potion restores sanity too.
